# Demo photo credits

The sample photographs used by `php artisan demo:install` are real Kerala wedding and engagement photographs from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/), published by their photographers under free licences.
They are bundled in `database/demo-images/` (resized to 2000 px on the long side, otherwise unmodified) so the demo installs offline.

**They are for previewing the website only.** `php artisan demo:remove` deletes them from the site. Before launch, replace them with the studio's own work.
If you ever keep one of these photographs on a public site you must credit the photographer and follow the licence
(CC BY: credit; CC BY-SA: credit and share adaptations under the same licence). While the demo is installed the credits are shown on the About page.

Some photographs carry the original photographer's small watermark (`jkproductions.in`); they are credited accordingly.
